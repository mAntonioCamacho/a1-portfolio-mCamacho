Motroid

Motroid is a 3D Horror Metroid-vania FPS Shooter vertical slice. You play as a no name character traversing through an abandoned space station fighting through quadrupedal beings, until reaching the BIG ONE.

This was a 5-7 week solo game project that utilized Unity's Visual Scripting and Nav Mesh tools. In this project I used game assets from the Unity Store, various 3D modeling websites, and various audio websites. The mechanics of the player and the enemies were all scripted by me of which are: player mechanics, enemy state behaviors, enemy animation transitions, audio scripting and more.

There is 1 unique enemy model that was used twice; one for basic enemies, and the other as the boss. The player has 4 mechanics(shoot, move, jump, jetpack), the basic enemy has 2 states(move and attack) while the boss has 4 states (move, attack, idle, dead). I used multiple audio tracks to create 3 different tracks that play in different gameplay states.
