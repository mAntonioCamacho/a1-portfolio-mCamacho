Samurai Cat

Samurai Cat is a 2D boss rush platforming game. You play as Sam the Samurai cat who is searching for fish to collect and eat. The problem is that the fish that he needs to collect were distributed amongst the Tyrant King's servants. Sam must defeat these servants and claim the fish.

Samurai Cat was developed by a 4 person group in under a 7 week time constraint. We had 1 artist, 2 technical game designers and 1 producer.

My role was a technical game designer and I co-designed and co-programmed the game's features such as the player movement and player mechanics. I designed and programmed the 2 bosses and both of their unique mechanics. When our time constraint became more apparent we settled for two bosses. When I finished with my side of the project, I took time to implement more 2D pixelated art.

Our end product was a 2 leveled, 2 boss, 2D boss rush platformer.
